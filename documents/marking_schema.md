# IT2140: Database Design and Development
**Assignment - Part 02 - Marking Schema**

| Criteria | Weight | Excellent | Good | Satisfactory | Poor | Very Poor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EER → Relational Schema Mapping** | 10 marks | **9-10:** All entities, PKs, FKs, constraints correctly mapped. ISA handled precisely with clear justification. | **7-8:** Mostly correct mappings with minor issues. Constraints are mostly accurate. | **5-6:** Basic mappings done with some missing PK/FK or ISA refinements. | **3-4:** Significant issues with mapping. Major PK/FK or ISA errors. | **0-2:** Very minimal or incorrect attempt with little evidence of understanding. |
| **SQL DDL Implementation** | 20 marks | **18-20:** Fully correct DDL with all constraints, accurate data types, consistent schema. | **15-17:** Minor syntax errors, most constraints implemented correctly. | **11-14:** Some missing constraints or mismatches between schema and DDL. | **6-10:** Major issues in DDL structure or constraints. | **0-5:** Very poor or incomplete implementation, schema not reflected. |
| **Insert Sample Data** | 10 marks | **9-10:** All tables populated with valid, consistent data. Clear screenshots provided. | **7-8:** Data is mostly valid with minor constraint issues or screenshot omissions. | **5-6:** Data inserted but lacks completeness or variety. | **3-4:** Incomplete or mostly invalid data, significant constraint violations. | **0-2:** No valid data inserted or screenshots missing. |
| **SQL Queries & Outputs** | 20 marks | **18-20:** All queries are correct, optimized, and well-explained. Outputs verified with clear screenshots. | **15-17:** Most queries are correct with minor logic or output issues. | **11-14:** Some queries incorrect or explanations unclear. | **6-10:** Major query errors, missing screenshots or explanations. | **0-5:** Very few valid queries provided or completely incorrect. |
| **Stored Function/Procedure** | 15 marks | **13-15:** Fully functional and relevant procedure/function with demonstration and correct output. | **11-12:** Minor logic issues but overall functional and relevant. | **8-10:** Partially correct, demonstration limited or incomplete. | **5-7:** Incorrect or poorly designed procedure/function. | **0-4:** No valid implementation provided. |
| **Trigger Implementation** | 15 marks | **13-15:** Fully functional trigger with correct logic and successful demonstration. | **11-12:** Minor issues but functional and relevant. | **8-10:** Basic trigger implemented but limited demonstration. | **5-7:** Incorrect or incomplete trigger with errors. | **0-4:** No valid trigger or irrelevant implementation. |
| **Viva & Demonstration (Individual)** | 10 marks | **9-10:** Accurately types and executes SQL queries during viva without assistance, with correct results and confident reasoning. | **7-8:** Successfully types and runs most queries with minor errors or assistance. | **5-6:** Can type and run basic queries but with noticeable frequent errors or incomplete execution. | **3-4:** Fails to correctly type or execute most queries, syntax or logic errors. | **0-2:** Cannot type or execute SQL queries during viva and unable to explain implementation. |
